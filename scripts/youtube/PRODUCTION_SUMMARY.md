## Resources & Tools Needed

**Must Have:**
- Quality microphone (USB condenser mic minimum)
- Video editing software (DaVinci Resolve free, or Premiere Pro)
- Royalty-free music license (Epidemic Sound, Artlist)
- Stock footage/images sources (Pexels, Pixabay, or paid)

**Nice to Have:**
- Camera for on-camera segments (smartphone works)
- Lighting setup (ring light or softbox)
- Teleprompter app for smoother delivery
- Graphics software (Canva or Adobe Suite)

**Budget Estimate:**
- Minimal setup: $100-300 (mic, free software)
- Professional setup: $1,000-3,000 (camera, lights, paid software)
- Stock footage subscription: $15-50/month
