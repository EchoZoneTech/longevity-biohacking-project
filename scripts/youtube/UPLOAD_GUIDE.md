## Content Guidelines & Best Practices

### Consistency
- Upload on a consistent schedule (weekly recommended to start)
- Maintain similar intro/outro structure
- Use consistent branding (colors, fonts, style)
- Keep quality standards high across all videos

### Engagement
- Ask viewers to comment specific questions
- Respond to comments within first 48 hours
- Create community posts between uploads
- Use YouTube Stories for quick tips
- Host occasional Q&A videos

### SEO Optimization
- Use keywords in titles naturally
- Front-load important keywords in descriptions
- Include relevant timestamps
- Use tags strategically (mix of broad and specific)
- Create custom thumbnails for all videos
- Use cards to link to related videos

### Safety & Ethics
- Always include medical disclaimers
- Present balanced view (benefits AND risks)
- Cite research sources
- Don't make medical claims
- Encourage viewers to consult healthcare providers
- Monitor comments for misinformation

---

## Analytics to Track

### Key Metrics
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Aim for 4-10% (good thumbnail/title combo)
- **Average View Duration:** Aim for 40-50%+ retention
- **Watch Time:** Total minutes watched (key for algorithm)
- **Subscriber Growth:** Track after each upload
- **Engagement Rate:** Likes, comments, shares per view

### Optimization Based on Data
- If CTR is low: Test new thumbnails/titles
- If retention drops at specific point: Tighten editing
- If engagement is low: Stronger calls-to-action
- Track which topics get most views: Create more similar content

---

## Legal & Copyright Considerations

### Music
- Use only royalty-free music or licensed music
- Recommended sources: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library
- Always credit in description if required

### Images/Video
- Use only licensed stock footage or create original
- Recommended stock sources: Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay (free), or Shutterstock, Storyblocks (paid)
- Never use copyrighted material without permission

### Research Citations
- Cite all studies mentioned in video
- Include links to research in description when possible
- Attribute quotes to original researchers
