## How to Use

### For Video Production

1. **Review the Script**: Read through the complete script to understand the flow and content
2. **Customize as Needed**: Adjust language, examples, or emphasis based on your personal style
3. **Prepare Visuals**: Use the visual notes to gather or create necessary B-roll, graphics, and animations
4. **Record**: Follow the script sections, using the voiceover text as your guide
5. **Edit**: Use timestamps and production notes to guide your editing process

### For Creating New Scripts

1. **Copy the Template**: Start with `script_template.md`
2. **Research Your Topic**: Gather scientific sources and credible information
3. **Fill in Sections**: 
   - Start with metadata (title, description, tags)
   - Write a compelling hook
   - Structure main content into logical sections
   - Create clear, actionable protocols
   - End with strong call-to-action
4. **Add Citations**: Include research sources for credibility
5. **Production Notes**: Document visual and audio requirements

## Script Structure Guidelines

### The Hook (First 5-10 seconds)
- Start with a compelling question or statement
- Capture attention immediately
- Tease the value viewers will get

### Introduction (30-60 seconds)
- Welcome viewers
- Clearly state what they'll learn
- Establish credibility

### Main Content (70-80% of video)
- Break into 3-5 clear sections
- Each section should have:
  - Scientific explanation
  - Practical protocol/application
  - Supporting research
- Use visuals to enhance understanding
- Keep language accessible but accurate

### Conclusion (Final 1-2 minutes)
- Recap key points
- Provide clear action steps
- Call-to-action (like, subscribe, comment)
- Teaser for next video (optional)

## Content Principles

### Science-Based
- Every claim should be backed by peer-reviewed research
- Cite studies from reputable institutions
- Acknowledge limitations and ongoing research

### Accessible
- Explain complex concepts in simple terms
- Use analogies and examples
- Avoid unnecessary jargon

### Actionable
- Provide specific protocols and steps
- Include safety considerations
- Make recommendations practical and realistic

### Engaging
- Tell stories when relevant
- Use compelling visuals
- Maintain good pacing
- Show enthusiasm for the topic

## Video Metadata Best Practices

### Titles
- Keep under 60 characters for full visibility
- Include main keyword early
- Make it compelling and clear
- Use numbers when applicable (e.g., "5 Ways to...")

### Descriptions
- First 2-3 sentences are most important (shown in search)
- Include relevant keywords naturally
- Add timestamps for better user experience
- Include calls-to-action and links

### Tags
- Use 8-15 relevant tags
- Include broad and specific terms
- Mix popular and niche keywords
- Focus on: longevity, biohacking, health optimization, anti-aging

## Production Tips

### Recording
- Use high-quality audio equipment
- Record in a quiet environment
- Speak clearly and with energy
- Record multiple takes of key sections

### Visuals
- Use a mix of on-camera and B-roll
- Include graphics for complex concepts
- Add text overlays for key points
- Use animations sparingly but effectively

### Editing
- Keep cuts dynamic (every 3-5 seconds during B-roll)
- Use background music at appropriate volume
- Add captions for accessibility
- Include end screen with next video suggestion
